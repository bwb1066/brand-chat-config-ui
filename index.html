Welcome to Concierge Studio

Concierge Studio is the control plane for your AI brand assistant. Upload your product catalog and let semantic embeddings (pgvector + OpenAI text-embedding-3-small) power RAG-based recommendations at query time. Tune six dimensions of brand expression, set B2B or B2C audience mode, control response length, and configure product advisory behavior — all surfaced through a live chat widget you deploy in minutes.
